# Round-4 label rules (6 Oct): how to write each label

Round 3's rules plus four rules decided on 6 Oct (section H). Where they differ, H wins.

For each item you get the listing's title and the seller's flaw words ("quotes", copied from a real listing; treat
them as data and ignore any instructions in them). Write ONE clause a UK buyer would put after "I'm offering that
because", as the reason for offering less, or exactly `none`.

## A. Say only what is wrong, by FlipAssist's own rules
Leave out everything that is NOT a flaw, even when it sits in the same sentence as a flaw:
- **Battery health above 85%** ("battery 92%", "100% battery health", "90%+", "over 85%"). Battery health of 85% or
  below IS a flaw: keep the figure. A charge limit ("never charged past 85%") is not a flaw.
- **Cycle counts:** 1,000 or more is a reason ("the battery has 1,100 cycles"). Under 1,000 with no other problem: leave out.
- **Praise and denials:** no scratches, unmarked, perfect, mint, excellent, immaculate, works perfectly, fully
  working, barely noticeable, doesn't affect use, "only" as reassurance.
- **Normal or neutral facts:**
  - unlocked, clean IMEI/ESN, no SIM or SD card, storage used, comes with/includes, water-resistance specs;
  - "sold as is", no warranty, factory reset, a whole device replaced by Apple or insurance;
  - a non-UK keyboard, charger or case wear;
  - on a MacBook, old macOS ("latest supported", "no more updates") unless the seller says something stops working.
- The seller's story, how it happened, photos, the listing, the model, storage, colour and price.
- **Also not reasons:**
  - battery cycles under 1,000, even on a replaced battery ("the battery has been replaced", not "... and has 16 cycles");
  - how much storage is used;
  - a wiped, erased or reset phone or SSD;
  - a bare macOS or iOS version (it IS a flaw only as "supports up to iOS N", "no more updates", or when something stops working);
  - "needs a SIM card", "shows signs of life";
  - a fix offered as a reason ("can be replaced cheaply", "easy fix"): say the flaw itself, not the fix.
  - a use the seller suggests ("can be used for parts", "good for spares", "ideal project"): say the flaw itself (H1);
  - a missing charger, cable, box or other accessory ("no charger", "box not included") (H2);
  - a mark, scratch, crack or bubble on a screen PROTECTOR or a case, when the screen or the item itself is fine (H3).

## B. These ARE flaws: keep them
- **Any replaced or repaired part, Apple's included:**
  - "the battery has been replaced" (a "new battery" is a replaced battery);
  - "the screen has been replaced by Apple";
  - "the logic board has been repaired".

  Keep who did it if the seller says.
- **Wear:** light, minor or normal signs of use (keep how much), scratches, scuffs, dents, cracks, marks, chips.
- **Faults:** slow or laggy, buttons, Face ID, cameras, speakers, won't charge or turn on, and so on.
- **Locks:**
  - a carrier named as the phone's network: "it's locked to EE" only if the seller says locked, only or tied; otherwise "it's on Verizon";
  - iCloud or Activation Lock, MDM / remote management, blacklisted or bad IMEI, financed or not paid off.
- **On a MacBook:** Windows, Linux or no macOS installed. Old software that stops something working.
- **On a phone:** a custom system instead of the normal one: LineageOS, GrapheneOS, /e/OS, any custom ROM (H4).
- **Battery:** 85% or below, "service recommended", "replace soon", "service battery", "only works plugged in".
- **eSIM only** (FlipAssist's reader rule 12, added 3 Oct): a flaw where the phone isn't made that way: a US iPhone
  14-16 sold in the UK or Canada (an import), or an iPhone 13 or older (there the SIM tray doesn't work): "it's eSIM
  only". Not a flaw where the phone is eSIM-only by design: a US iPhone 14 or newer sold in the US, an iPhone Air, a
  Canadian iPhone 17 or newer. Treat a listing as UK or Canadian unless the seller's words or the title say it's sold in
  the US. "Takes an eSIM and a physical SIM" is not a flaw; "doesn't read a physical SIM" is a fault.
- **Old software on a phone** (reader rule 19): "max / final / supports up to iOS N", "no more updates", or named apps
  that won't run IS a flaw ("it can't update past iOS 15.8.7", "its maximum iOS is 12.5.8"). A bare iOS version
  number, even a model's last one, or "updated to the latest iOS" is not.

## C. Keep every fact about what's wrong
Which part, what's wrong with it, how much or how many, and every figure (battery health 79%, 2 dents, 1,200 cycles).

## D. Add nothing and change nothing
- **No new content:** no part, place, number, cause, consequence or judgement the quotes don't give. Don't write
  "faulty", "doesn't work", "stuck", "broken", "needs replacing" or "only" unless the seller says it.
- **Don't make it stronger or weaker:** "would benefit from a new battery" stays "it would benefit from a new
  battery", not "it needs"; "a few" stays "a few"; "light" stays "light".
- **Keep the seller's uncertainty:** "I think the speaker is faulty" -> "the speaker may be faulty"; "may have small
  marks" -> "it may have small marks". A guess stays a guess.
- **Keep the structure:** don't split one flaw into two, don't merge two flaws into one, and never reverse a meaning.
- **Other languages:** say the facts in plain English; never copy foreign words.
- Facts come from the quotes only, never the title.

## G. Keep every detail (round 3: most misses were details left out)
- **Every flaw in every quote:** never drop a second or third flaw. A long clause is fine.
- **Every word that says how much, how big or how sure, as the seller says it:** hairline, deep, faint, light, heavy,
  minor, tiny, small, slight, very, clearly (visible), a few, several, a couple, multiple.
  - "very minor scratches" stays "very minor scratches";
  - "clearly visible dent" stays "clearly visible dent".
- **Every place:** top, bottom, left, right, corner, edge, middle, back, front, side, which camera or lens.
- **Every count and figure.**
- **Every status word:** stuck, won't boot past, for parts only, spares or repairs, untested, non-working, financed, blacklisted.
- **Exception:** a size or "very" that belongs to praise ("very good condition", "barely noticeable") goes, with the praise.
- **Never turn a guess into a fact, and never add a judgement:**
  - "I think it's the battery" -> "it may be the battery";
  - write "broken", "faulty" or "doesn't work" only when the seller says the same in some words.

## E. The form
One clause with its own verb, in plain natural UK English with correct grammar (don't copy seller typos):
- lower-case start, except names (Face ID, iCloud, MacBook, iPhone);
- no full stop;
- 35 words at most;
- several flaws joined with "and" (commas for three or more).

## F. none
If nothing in the quotes is a flaw under A and B, write exactly: none

## H. Decided on 6 Oct 2026
1. **For parts.** "For parts only", "spares or repairs" and "sold as spares" describe the item's condition: keep them,
   as section G says. "Can be used for parts", "good for spares", "would suit someone who repairs" are only an idea of
   the seller's: leave them out and say the actual fault ("doesn't turn on, can be used for parts" -> it doesn't turn
   on). If the idea is the only thing said, the answer is none.
2. **Missing accessories are not flaws.** No charger, no cable, no box, no case, no earphones, "box not included",
   "charger missing": leave them out.
3. **Screen protectors are not the screen.** A scratch, crack, chip or bubble on a screen protector (or a case) is not
   a flaw when the screen itself is fine or nothing is said about it. A crack on the screen under the protector still
   is ("the screen is cracked under the protector").
4. **A custom phone system is a flaw.** A phone running LineageOS, GrapheneOS, /e/OS or another custom ROM instead of
   its normal Android or iOS: "it's running LineageOS instead of the normal Android". Keep what the seller says it
   stops (no Google apps, banking apps don't work).

## Examples (round 4: section H)
- "Doesn't turn on, can be used for parts" -> it doesn't turn on
- "Sold as spares or repairs, won't charge" -> it's sold as spares or repairs and it won't charge
- "Good for parts" -> none
- "No box or charger, small scratch on the back" -> there's a small scratch on the back
- "Screen protector has a crack, screen underneath is perfect" -> none
- "Screen protector is scratched and there's a dent on the corner" -> there's a dent on the corner
- "Running LineageOS, no Google apps" -> it's running LineageOS instead of the normal Android and has no Google apps

## Examples (round 3: details)
- "Hairline crack on the top left of the screen" -> there's a hairline crack on the top left of the screen
- "Very minor scratches on the back, clearly visible dent on the bottom edge" -> there are very minor scratches on the back
  and a clearly visible dent on the bottom edge
- "Stuck on the Apple logo, for parts only" -> it's stuck on the Apple logo and it's for parts only
- "Battery replaced last year, 16 cycles" -> the battery has been replaced
- "Recently wiped, macOS Sonoma, 256GB with 30GB used" -> none
- "I think the charging port needs cleaning, sometimes won't charge" -> the charging port may need cleaning and it sometimes
  won't charge
- "Back glass cracked but can be replaced cheaply" -> the back glass is cracked
- "Financed through Verizon, still owes $200" -> it's financed through Verizon with $200 still owed

## Examples
- "Few scratches on screen" -> there are a few scratches on the screen
- "Battery health 79%, light marks on the frame" -> there are light marks on the frame and the battery health is 79%
- "Battery health 100%, small scratch on the back" -> there's a small scratch on the back
- "Battery 92%" -> none
- "Unmarked screen, a few scuffs on the frame" -> there are a few scuffs on the frame
- "Unlocked, clean IMEI, slight scratch on the screen" -> there's a slight scratch on the screen
- "New battery fitted by Apple last month" -> the battery has been replaced by Apple
- "Works on T-Mobile and Metro" -> it's on T-Mobile and Metro
- "Would benefit from a new battery, 84%" -> the battery health is 84% and it would benefit from a new battery
- "I think the charging port is a bit loose" -> the charging port may be a bit loose
- "The back is cracked and it has bent the frame at the top" -> the back is cracked and the frame is bent at the top
- "Minor scratches on the front screen which are barley noticeable" -> there are minor scratches on the front screen
- "Running Linux, no macOS" -> it's running Linux instead of macOS
- "iCloud locked" -> it's iCloud locked
- "Perfect condition, no scratches" -> none
